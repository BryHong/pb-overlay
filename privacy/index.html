Privacy policy

Your match footage

PB Overlay processes your match on your iPhone or iPad. When you pick a video from Photos, the app receives a copy of the video you select. When you record in the app, it uses the camera and microphone to capture picture and sound.

The app analyzes video frames and player movement on your device to find rallies. It saves trimmed video, clip times and match statistics in the app. We do not receive your footage or movement data, except the rally fix clips described below, which are sent only if you agree. The app works without an account and contains no advertising or third-party analytics tools.

Rally fixes

If a rally in your video is cut short, you can tap Cut off? to add the missing seconds. If one starts too early, you can tap Slow start? where it really begins, and if a clip isn’t a rally, you can tap Not a rally? to take it out. When you first open the app, we ask whether PB Overlay may also send us a clip of about 20 seconds around each rally you fix. You can say no, and you can stop sending at any time in Settings.

If you agree, each fix sends that clip, the rally times before and after your fix (or that you marked it not a rally), the app’s rally detection results for those seconds, summary statistics of the match’s motion and image features, the app version, when you agreed, and a random number created on your device. It doesn’t include your name, Apple Account or the rest of your video. Clips are sent over Wi-Fi to Apple’s CloudKit service for PB Overlay. By agreeing, you confirm that everyone in your videos has agreed to be filmed and to these uses.

We use fix clips only to train and test PB Overlay’s rally detection. We review them ourselves, don’t sell or share them, and keep them while they’re useful for training. To have clips you sent deleted, email the address below with the sender ID shown in the app’s Settings, under Rally fixes.

When you share

If you pair phones to record a match, they exchange camera identifiers, preview images, device status and timing information over a local connection. The second camera sends its rally clips directly to the main phone.

Save to Photos adds a copy to your photo library. Photos may sync that copy according to your iCloud settings. If you use Share, the app or person you select receives the file you choose.

Storage and deletion

Saved matches stay in the app until you delete them. In Matches, open the match menu and choose Delete Match. To remove a full recording while keeping its trimmed video, use the storage controls in Settings.

Deleting PB Overlay removes its local app data. Manage copies in Photos, backups or paired devices separately. You can change camera, microphone, photo and local network permissions in your device’s Settings.
