Privacy policy

Your match footage

PB Overlay processes your match on your iPhone or iPad. When you pick a video from Photos, the app receives a copy of the video you select. When you record in the app, it uses the camera and microphone to capture picture and sound.

The app analyzes video frames and player movement on your device to find rallies. It saves trimmed video, clip times and match statistics in the app. We do not receive your footage or movement data. The app works without an account and contains no advertising or third-party analytics tools.

When you share

If you pair phones to record a match, they exchange camera identifiers, preview images, device status and timing information over a local connection. The second camera sends its rally clips directly to the main phone.

Save to Photos adds a copy to your photo library. Photos may sync that copy according to your iCloud settings. If you use Share, the app or person you select receives the file you choose.

Storage and deletion

Saved matches stay in the app until you delete them. In Matches, open the match menu and choose Delete Match. To remove a full recording while keeping its trimmed video, use the storage controls in Settings.

Deleting PB Overlay removes its local app data. Manage copies in Photos, backups or paired devices separately. You can change camera, microphone, photo and local network permissions in your device’s Settings.
