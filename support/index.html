Using PB Overlay

Where is my trimmed video?

Open Matches and select your match. Tap Save to Photos to put a copy in your photo library. For an imported match, the original stays in Photos.

How do I share one rally?

Open the match and find the rally in the Clips list. Touch and hold its row, then choose Share Rally.

Can I keep more time around each point?

Open Settings and adjust Before the serve or After the point. The new settings apply to the next video you trim.

What does “Check this one” mean?

PB Overlay kept a clip it was less certain about. Tap the clip to watch it and check where the point starts and ends.
